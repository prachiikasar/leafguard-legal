🌿 LeafGuard AI

Privacy Policy · Effective August 23, 2026

Introduction

LeafGuard AI is an offline plant disease detection app. We designed it with privacy in mind: your photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. No account is required. We do not transmit your photos or scan results to external servers.

Camera & Gallery

The app uses camera and gallery access only when you start a scan. Images are analyzed locally with on-device AI (TensorFlow Lite).

On-Device Processing

All machine learning runs on your phone. No cloud AI services are used for diagnosis.

Local Storage

Scan history is saved on your device so you can review past results. You can delete scans individually or clear all history. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

Internet Access

The app may download font files (Google Fonts) for typography. This does not send your photos or personal data. Disease detection works offline.

Third-Party Services

We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs. The google_fonts package may contact Google Fonts to download fonts.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
